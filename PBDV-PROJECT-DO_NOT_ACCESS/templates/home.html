Get to Know Tute Me: Your Gateway to Smarter Learning!

Peer Tutoring is a platform that connects students with peer tutors for personalized learning experiences. Our mission is to make education more accessible and personalized through peer-to-peer learning.

Empowering students through personalized peer-to-peer learning. Schedule sessions, track progress, and connect with tutors who make learning simpler, smarter, and more effective.

Who’s Who?

Students find tutors and book sessions, tutors manage schedules and teach, while admins keep everything running smoothly.

Seamless Scheduling

Students book sessions based on tutor availability, tutors accept or decline, and automated reminders prevent missed sessions.

Top-Tier Tutors

Tutors are verified for expertise, students leave reviews for transparency, and admins ensure high-quality learning.
